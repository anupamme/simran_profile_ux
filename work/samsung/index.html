Shaping Samsung's first Tizen phone and Bixby for South Asian users

Foundational, evaluative and iterative research in an agile product environment. It covered the Samsung Z1 and Tizen OS for India, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka, Bixby, and a range of AI, health and wearables projects across cultural contexts.

What does a first smartphone, and a first voice assistant, need to do for people in India, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka?

The question

The Samsung Z1 was Samsung's first commercial Tizen OS smartphone, a low-cost phone for the Indian subcontinent. Product teams needed to know which features and experiences would matter to those users. Later, they needed to know how a context-aware voice assistant (Bixby) and other AI agents should behave across cultural contexts.

My role

As a Senior UX Researcher in the NXT Gen Lab, I was responsible for foundational, evaluative and iterative research on the Z1 and Tizen OS. I defined and led UX research processes across Samsung Health, Wearables, Human-AI Trust, Voice Assistants and Autonomous Agents. That work spanned trends and future projections, user research, information design, usability, visualisation and presentations.

Methods

  • Foundational field research
  • Usability testing
  • In-depth interviews
  • Diary studies
  • Intercept surveys
  • Market research
  • Trend & future-scenario research

Who I worked with

I worked alongside design, engineering and management teams for cross-functional delivery inside Samsung's matrixed R&D organisation. I presented findings to senior leadership and cross-functional stakeholders in India, Korea and the US.

The research also looked at human–AI trust and computer-mediated communication, and what makes people comfortable letting an assistant act for them. That work shaped how Bixby and the autonomous-agent concepts should speak and behave.

Impact

  • Shipped: the Samsung Z1 and Tizen OS launched in India, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka. The project won the Samsung Excellence Award for Outstanding Project of the Year (2015).
  • Bixby: I translated research into design decisions within sprint cycles for Bixby's integration into Samsung phones as a context-aware assistant.
  • Samsung Fold: I combined market research, intercept surveys, interviews and diary studies to help define the positioning for the Fold technology and prototype.
