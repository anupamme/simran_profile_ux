Case study · MoSaIC: More-than-Human Smart Cities · City St George's, University of London & UAL · 2024–present

Aligning government, industry and community partners on smart-city services

MoSaIC is a £1.6M UKRI project with DEFRA and Arup. I lead research that informs service-design and policy decisions, and I'm setting up an in-situ field site where those decisions can be tested with the people and places they affect.

How do you get a government department, an engineering firm and community organisations to agree on what a city should do?

The question
