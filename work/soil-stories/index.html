Turning sensor data into stories people act on, with LLM-generated narratives

A device that turns live soil readings into short AI-written stories. I used it as a probe to learn how people interpret, trust and respond to generated narratives compared with raw data.

Would people care more about their soil if it could tell them a story?

The question

Sensor dashboards rarely change behaviour. Can LLM-generated narratives make environmental data meaningful enough to act on, and what do people need in order to trust a story written by an AI?
