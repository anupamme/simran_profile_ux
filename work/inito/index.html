Redesigning onboarding for an at-home fertility monitor to 89% task completion

End-to-end research for a health diagnostics device, from ideation to implementation, with in-home usability studies that shaped the product ahead of its production launch.

My role
Design Research Consultant. I scoped, designed and ran the research end to end
Type
  • Evaluative
Product
Inito Fertility Monitor: a smartphone-connected at-home hormone test
Result
89% task completion on the redesigned onboarding

Can someone set up a medical diagnostics device at home, alone, the first time, and trust the result?

The question

Before production launch, the team needed to know where people struggled to set up and use the device at home, and what had to change so that first-time users could complete onboarding successfully.
