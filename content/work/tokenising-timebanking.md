## The question

Time banks run on reciprocity, but keeping track of who gave how much time, and to whom, is administrative work that falls on volunteers and coordinators. The project asked how new technologies could reduce that work, and help volunteers track and exchange their time for help from their community.

## How time banking works

Time banking is a form of community exchange where people trade time-based services rather than money. For every hour of service someone gives, such as tutoring, caregiving or household repairs, they earn one time credit, which they can spend on help from another member. It builds social cohesion and supports underserved people through mutual aid.

## Methods
