It is an ongoing research and development endeavour. It is in continuation of my masters thesis project where I explored food politics through self initiated research. The research manifested into numerous personal and professional outcomes such as publications in international conferences, a self sustaining planter, series of food activism artworks & installations presented internationally and community uncookbook on facebook.

Travelling further on the road I started growing some of my own food, through organic urban farming. I ventured out into my own edible terrace garden which inspired me to again merge technology and the urban farming. This endeavour lead me to develop a prototype of a smart planter which can monitor growth of plants through a series of sensors and applications. I disembarked from the project as I learned the intricacies of organic farming and found the project to be trivial in nature when compared to the monetary or intrinsic value. After having seen the work of Open Agricultures Lab recently, I am now trying to build a frugal aeroponic structure with the help of a makerspace in Bangalore.

In my upcoming work I would want to look at disrupting urban living spaces and systems to reconfigure the urban life towards more ecological point of view which can define the future of not only food and living spaces but also break the paradox of choice. Making technology and food accessible to everyone.
