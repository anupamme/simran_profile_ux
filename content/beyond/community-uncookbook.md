It is a community on facebook that was started after a food residency at Khoj Artists Association, New Delhi, India. Like minded friends with concerns about food got together and decided to reach out to many more people through social media. We developed a manifesto and started the community on the lines of health, organic, minimally processed food on the lines of raw and vegan. We wanted people to be able to have access to alternative food cultures that talk about health, nutrition, lifestyle to question existing food systems. Helping people make informed choices about the food that they put in their bodies.

Community Uncookbook can be found online through this link: [Facebook Community](https://www.facebook.com/groups/communityuncookbook/)
