Origin / metamorphosis

A short animation made on the theme of origin or metamorphosis. Taking inspiration from the movie Powers of Ten by Charles & Ray Eames I wanted to start and end with a Dot (Bindu) hence the name of the animation. The Dot (Bindu) progresses, transforms and morphs into different life forms in nature giving a feeling of origin or beginning.

Medium: Clay & Paper  
Stop motion animation; 350 frames
