Classroom project NTADBM 2011 where IIM & NID had collaborated.

Flush is a toilet finder mobile application, a service that helps you find the nearest public toilet. The application was born from insights of daily problems of people in urban India. There is general lack of proper sanitation and availability of toilets in most public places in India. Also information about the kind and condition of the toilet is missing. This is due to improper signage and varied range available: western, Indian, urinal, compost pits, etc.

It’s a GPS based app, which narrows down where you are with just one click and gives the address of toilets within a given radius based upon geomapping. The service is available on smartphones through application and other phones through SMS service.The application can be downloaded from the internet and registered users can rate each toilet on a five-star scale and give comments and reviews. Users can also add new locations with toilets from their mobile device, take photos of the facilities and upload it directly to the online portal. Search options for specific queries can be made like Indian style or western, paid or unpaid, type of location (mall, restaurant, etc) and availability of handicap facilities.

The application is mainly targeted at women and the tourism sector. The navigation and the GUI of the app has been designed keeping specifically to this target group.
