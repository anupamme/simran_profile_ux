Remediation of the painting palette, trying to incorporate the real life painting experiences on Tablets through the technological capabilities of the medium. The design brief by Adobe was to look at redefining the tools in an already existing desktop based format to that of a tablet. People are using the Tablet to create illustrations, artworks, graphics and presentations. The thought process lay in the domain of creating new tools, exploiting the additional capabilities of the medium (built in sensors, touch), optimizing interactions with a natural experience which was an amalgamation of real and virtual.

Remediation of the painting palette tries to incorporate the real life painting experience into the digital world. It provides capabilities of the touch for direct manipulation of paint and familiar painting gestures giving the user natural immersion. Simple design principles were also kept in mind while designing the interface. The mixing area of the palette can manipulate color, transparency and consistency. Consistency and transparency are interdependent in the real painting experience but can be separated in the digital environment. With a natural simple finger rotation gesture, like a brush on a palette the consistency and transparency can be changed. The palette also uses the capabilities of the gyroscope in the tablet to further enhance the experience of painting on tablets like dripping of paint.
