Food, today, is inextricably caught up in a web of politics, controversy, and host of issues, the emergence of which can be traced back to the industry, media and corporation led practices of production and distribution, fortified by mindless consumption in the urban setting.

Looking at the overall landscape of this issue, an experimental intervention was devised taking the form of participatory game-based installation. It was conceived as part of the residency program InContext:public.art.ecology at Khoj International Artists Association, New Delhi. The objective was to initiate a public dialogue around food issues through art and design interventions.

The allegorical play offers an insight into the mindless culture of consumption, in context to contemporary food and purchasing practices. The game mechanics in it itself carries the message. In a limited number of turns and moves that the player must negotiate, the game play attempts to explain the journey as a consumer confronted with the paradox of choice in everyday life. The tendency of people to blindly follow rules in a game is metaphorical to the real life scenario, that we find ourselves embroiled in. Intentional missing explanations induce complicity and some hard hitting truths are unearthed towards the end, inducing staggering reactions.
