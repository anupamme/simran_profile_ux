Entree is a project that, through the lens of experimental media, intends to launch an investigation into what the technologically riddled future of a meal could entail, be it the far future, or the very next month.

Food can be regarded as a medium that constantly connects time, space and memories of a world that one grew up in to that in which one will grow old. In a manner that is both tangible and intangible, it links the present to the past and transcends into the future.

Post the era of industrialization, the production, distribution, and consumption of food are and always have been troubled with complex issues ranging from industrialization to globalization. Wherein the food we eat and closely associate ourselves with, is marked by its own journey through the road of cultural, industrial, and ecological changes. With traces of chemical elements, toxic materials, and other unknowns in consumables, it is the meal in this post-media age that has been put under the scanner in this project.

The hyper-mediated experience addresses the painful truth of reality by embracing the blissful ignorance of illusion, created by the presence of choice. In a world inundated with choice, the mechanical and automated nature of the meal embodies the paradox of choicelessness, given the prison of consumption which our bodies are born into. The space, in its unfeeling morbidness seeks to recreate an imagery, of this very prison, a warped reflection of our lives, that one can realize or simply overlook.The desire to exit and return to the reality is invoked, but not without leaving one questioning about the real and the surreal, fact and fiction, with respect to what we commonly perceive as 'food' and nourishment.
