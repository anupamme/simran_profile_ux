Dream of a Digital Camera

Do robots dream of mechanical sheep? A classroom exercise to select a machine and depict what the machine will dream about. I made an experimental animation showing, what will the nightmare of a Digital Camera look like. Inspired from a mechanical ballet, I played around with a limited number of shapes, colours and photography techniques.

Medium: Hand painted cardboard cutouts  
Stop motion animation; 450 frames
